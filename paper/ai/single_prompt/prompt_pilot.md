Compute the echoing period Δ of the Choptuik critical solution in D = 4, 6, 7 and 8 spacetime dimensions, each correct to six decimal places.

The critical solution is the discretely self-similar solution of general relativity with a spherically symmetric, minimally coupled, massless real scalar field that sits at the threshold of black hole formation. Δ is its period in logarithmic time.

Work from first principles in the current directory: derive the equations, write the solver, and test your own results for convergence. Do not look the values up, and do not quote them from memory. Python 3 with numpy, scipy, mpmath and sympy is installed. You have 4 CPU threads.

When you are done, write two files:
- results.csv, with the header D,Delta and one row for each D;
- METHOD.md, which says how each value was computed and what its convergence test showed.
